    Learned Vocabulary
    Learned
    Words the app learns by watching what you correct. When you fix the same word in 2 separate edits of pasted text (before hitting Enter), it is remembered: Whisper gets it as a hint, the refinement model sees "heard → intended", and close misspellings are also replaced automatically. Click × to remove anything wrong. It then stops being used.
    Pending corrections — seen once; they move up to Learned after 2 separate edits and expire after 90 days

    What you can do

    Download it yourself

    Which one do I need?

    1. Click "Open download page" of the package you need. Your browser downloads the .zip file.
    2. When it has finished, click "Use downloaded file…" and pick that .zip file. Trispr Flow checks it against the fixed fingerprint before it uses it.

    Download the speech model

    The speech model turns your voice into text. It is downloaded once and then runs fully offline. You can continue with the next steps while it downloads.
